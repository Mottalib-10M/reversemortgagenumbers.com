These Mortgagee Letters provide the mortgage limits for Title II FHA-insured forward mortgages and the maximum claim amount for FHA-insured HECMs for Calendar Year (CY) 2026. 2026 Nationwide Forward Mortgage Limits FHA's nationwide forward mortgage limit "floor" and "ceiling" for a one-unit property in CY 2026 are $541,287 and $1,249,125, respectively. Select the links below for additional mortgage limits guidance for forward mortgages: FHA Mortgage Limits Search Areas at Ceiling Areas Between Floor and Ceiling Jurisdictions with Loan Limit Decreases Refer to Mortgagee Letter 2025-23 for more details. 2026 Nationwide HECM Limits For CY 2026, the HECM nationwide maximum claim amount will be $1,249,125 for all areas, and effective for all case numbers assigned on or after January 1, 2026, through December 31, 2026.
